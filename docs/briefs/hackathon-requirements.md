# #AI4Elections Hackathon: what the organisers asked for

From the onboarding session on 21 Nov 2026 (Rise Networks with INEC; partners MacArthur Foundation, Luminate,
Microsoft, Google, Meta). 80 teams registered across 5 tracks. Use this when building the slides, the problem
statement and the submission.

## Objectives

- Practical AI solutions to defined electoral problems
- Multidisciplinary teamwork
- Responsible, nonpartisan innovation

## Expected impact

- A stronger ecosystem for electoral technology
- Research and policy insight
- A lasting national community

## What teams must achieve

- Validate a real problem
- Build and test a working prototype
- Document limits and safeguards
- Present to judges

## The five tracks

| Track | Problem teams address |
|---|---|
| 1. AI and Trustworthy Electoral Information | Manipulated or AI-generated electoral content |
| **2. Electoral Data Analysis, Operational Insights and Decision Support** | **Data quality and anomalies flagged for humans** (our track) |
| 3. Inclusive and Multilingual/Civic Tech | Barriers of language, disability and access |
| 4. (named like track 3 on the slide; the problem is security) | Defensive security and privacy for electoral tech |
| 5. Election Observation and Citizen Accountability | Safe incident reporting and public information |

Where AI4Elections also touches the others: 3 (answers in Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Pidgin), 4 (RLS, MFA, audit chain,
privacy), 5 (observer sheet photos, claims kept unverified until a second person checks evidence).

## Team rules

- Two to four members with mixed skills
- Roles: team lead, technical lead, research lead, design and docs
- Agree responsibilities early, track contributions, resolve disagreements respectfully
- Attend sessions, join check-ins, flag blockers early
- Team membership is locked once the hackathon begins

## Deliverables (due by 13 Dec 2026)

| Stage | What to hand in | Where we have it |
|---|---|---|
| Design | Problem statement; users and solution design | Pitch brief (problem); user guide; roles table; "how the parts fit" diagram. To do: one-page problem statement |
| Develop | Working prototype or credible proof of concept; technical documentation | Live app; README, runbook, OpenAPI contract, phase reports |
| Test | Evidence it works; honest limitations | Live checks (security, ingestion, real Plateau 2023 data, assistant, sheet reader); 84% on 20 real EC8A sheets; known limits in the responsible-AI brief |
| Submit | Safeguards and risks; presentation and demo | Responsible-AI and security brief. To do: slide deck, recorded demo |

Idea-only entries and mock-ups are not eligible.
