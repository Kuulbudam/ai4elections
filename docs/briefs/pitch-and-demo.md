# AI4Elections: pitch, demo script and backup video

## One line

**AI4Elections helps observer groups and newsrooms turn polling-unit results into evidence that holds up: it checks
the data, reads result-sheet photos, compares collation totals, and takes every anomaly through human review to a
signed-off report.**

## How it differs from what exists

Existing tools such as public result dashboards show one organisation's analysis of published results. AI4Elections
is a **workflow any team can run on its own data**, and its output is defensible evidence, not a chart:

- **from anomaly to evidence:** every flag is reviewed by a person, explained or escalated, with the reasoning kept;
- **photos and collation, not just spreadsheets:** it reads the result sheet itself and checks the declared totals;
- **tamper-evident:** fingerprinted files, append-only records and a hash-chained audit log;
- **responsible by design:** never says fraud, labels allegations as unverified, and works without AI;
- **inclusive:** answers in Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo and Pidgin.

## 3-minute pitch

1. **Problem (25 s).** After every election, observers have photos of result sheets, spreadsheets of polling-unit
   figures and declared totals that don't add up, but no shared, trustworthy way to check them. Claims spread
   faster than evidence.
2. **Product (20 s).** AI4Elections: upload, validate, analyse, review, report, with AI where it saves time and a
   person wherever a judgement is made.
3. **Live demo (2 min).** The script is below.
4. **Why us, what next (15 s).** Working today on real 2023 Plateau data. On 20 real handwritten result sheets the AI
   read 84% of figures exactly, and flagged most of the rest for a person. Next: pilots with observer groups and newsrooms.

## Demo script (about 2 minutes, from the prepared demo project)

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Dashboard → project | "This is a real 2023 Plateau governorship file: about 5,000 polling units." |
| 0:10 | Validation | "Before any analysis, every row is checked: missing figures, accredited above registered, sums that don't add up." |
| 0:25 | Analysis dashboard | "Each unit is compared with similar units in its ward. These are the unusual ones. Unusual, not wrong." |
| 0:40 | Result sheets → upload the mismatch sample | "An observer sends a photo of the sheet. The AI reads every figure in seconds and marks what it's unsure of." |
| 0:55 | Confirm | "A person checks it against the photo and confirms. Now the platform checks the sheet's own sums and compares it with the data. Fifteen extra votes, flagged." |
| 1:15 | Collation check → upload sample | "Declared ward totals against the sum of their polling units: three areas differ, each sent for review." |
| 1:30 | Finding → review → claim | "Every flag gets a decision and a reason, kept for ever. An allegation stays unverified until someone else verifies it with evidence." |
| 1:45 | Ask the data (Hausa) | "Staff can ask in plain language, here in Hausa. Every figure links back to its source." |
| 1:55 | Report | "One click: a report with a fingerprint and an audit checkpoint, ready to publish." |

Leave about 30 seconds between AI steps (free plan). If the AI is busy, show that the figures can be typed in and
every check still runs.
